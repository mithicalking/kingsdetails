Privacy Policy

How MK Car Details collects, uses, and protects your information.

Last updated: June 2026

Overview

We are amobile car detailing service based in Denton, TX. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website or contact us to book a service, and how we use it.

We keep it simple — we only collect what we need to do our job, and we never sell your information.

Information We Collect

When you reach out to book a detail or ask a question, we may collect:

  • Your name
  • Phone number and/or email address
  • Vehicle information (make, model, condition)
  • Your general location or address for mobile service scheduling

We do not collect payment information directly — any payments are processed through third-party services (such as Venmo or Cashapp) that have their own privacy policies.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely to:

  • Confirm and schedule your appointment
  • Contact you about your booking or follow up after service
  • Send occasional updates if you've opted in (you can opt out anytime)

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not share it with third parties except as required to complete your service (e.g., scheduling tools).

Cookies & Website Data

Our website does not use tracking cookies or analytics tools that collect personal data. If this changes in the future, we will update this policy accordingly. However, Our booking page uses Celendly which has its own privacy policy.

Your Information, Your Control

Your information is deleted 30 days after your last detailing session.
